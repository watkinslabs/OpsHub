# S140 — Restore and purge

## Identity

- Parent feature: `F070` Trash and recovery
- Owner: platform
- Branch: `s140-restore-and-purge`
- Decision references: `docs/architecture-decisions.md` sections 2, 2.1, 3, 4, 6, 7; `docs/capability-contracts.md` row F070; `docs/authorization-model.md` section 3.3; `docs/threat-model.md` sections 3.4 and 3.6

## Vertical slice

As an editor, I want a screen that shows what was deleted with the time left before it is gone and puts it back in one click, and as a compliance administrator I want to destroy one item now with the same care a governance purge takes, so that recovery is self-service and destruction is deliberate, audited and refused while a legal hold stands.

This slice delivers the `/trash` screen, the retention countdown and expiry sweep, the legal-hold refusal, purge-now through the shared F027 executor, and the recovery evidence suite that proves all of it.

## Requirements

- **SR-S140-01:** The `/trash` route renders `TrashPage` with `TrashFilters`, `TrashTable` and `TrashRow` over `DataGridPanel`, showing kind icon, title, original location, deleter, deleted time, days remaining and state, and ships the loading, empty, error with `correlation_id`, denied-as-empty, stale, offline and success states from the F062 patterns (covers FR-F070-12, NFR-F070-03).
- **SR-S140-02:** `RestoreDialog` names the item, its destination path and the child count the owning feature will bring back, restores on confirm, invalidates the trash and the owning list query keys, and toasts `Open`; a blocked row shows `BlockedReason` with `Restore parent first` linking to the parent's entry instead of the dialog (covers FR-F070-07, FR-F070-12).
- **SR-S140-03:** `expires_at` is `deleted_at` plus the F027 `purge_after_days` for the kind read through `RetentionPolicyPort`, a null policy renders as no countdown, and `days_remaining` is computed against the request clock in the tenant's timezone (covers FR-F070-08).
- **SR-S140-04:** The nightly `trash.sweep` job marks entries past `expires_at` as `expired` and hands 500 per tenant per batch to `PurgeExecutorPort`; it hard-deletes nothing itself and skips any entry whose owning row is no longer deleted (covers FR-F070-08, NFR-F070-04).
- **SR-S140-05:** A legal hold beats the policy everywhere: `LegalHoldPort::is_held` is consulted before every purge, a held entry is marked `held` with `state = 'held'`, survives past `expires_at`, is skipped and counted by the sweep, and refuses `DELETE` with `409 conflict` code `legal_hold` naming the hold, while restore of a held item still succeeds (covers FR-F070-09).
- **SR-S140-06:** `DELETE /api/v1/trash/{kind}/{id}` requires `compliance-admin` plus `Idempotency-Key` and `If-Match` on `source_version`, runs through `PurgeExecutorPort` so the F027 audited path executes, removes the owning row and its declared blobs, writes the `trash.purge` audit event, publishes `item.purged.v1`, and returns `204`; an `editor` receives `403 denied` and `PurgeDialog` is disabled with the reason (covers FR-F070-10, NFR-F070-02).
- **SR-S140-07:** `PurgeDialog` is the destructive variant, states that the action cannot be undone, requires retyping the item title before the action enables, traps focus, announces the result, and returns focus to the originating row (covers FR-F070-10, NFR-F070-03).
- **SR-S140-08:** The performance lane proves the 400 ms p95 first page over 200,000 entries, the 5 s p95 and 120 s p99 projection lag, and the 3-minute rebuild, and the requirements lane traces every FR-F070 and NFR-F070 id to a case (covers NFR-F070-01, NFR-F070-05).
